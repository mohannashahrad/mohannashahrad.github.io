I am a Ph.D. candidate advised by Prof. [Mae Milano](https://www.languagesforsyste.ms) in the [Princeton SNS Group](https://sns.cs.princeton.edu/). My research is in distributed systems and cloud computing. I'm excited about developing cloud-native programming paradigms that enable cloud systems that are more adaptive and performance-aware.

Before starting my Ph.D., I spent four wonderful years completing my undergraduate degree at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) in Montreal, where I was advised by Prof. [Bettina Kemme](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~kemme/) for my [NSERC USRA](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/ug-pc/usra-brpc_eng.asp) project, in which I studied the use of views in graph database systems. In my senior year, I spent an exchange semester at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/), where I was supervised by Prof. Karthik Pattabiraman in the [Dependable Systems Lab](https://blogs.ubc.ca/dependablesystemslab/). During my time there, I worked on a information flow control framework for managing privacy in IoT applications.
